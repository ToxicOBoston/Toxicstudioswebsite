Profile

Toxic O is a South African trapper, songwriter, and music composer known for his unique blend of trap and hip-hop music. Rising to prominence in the mid-2020s, Toxic O has captivated audiences with his introspective lyrics and innovative soundscapes. His music often explores themes of personal struggle, ambition, and societal issues, resonating deeply with a diverse fanbase. With several successful singles and albums under his belt, He continues to push the boundaries of contemporary music while maintaining a strong connection to his roots.
